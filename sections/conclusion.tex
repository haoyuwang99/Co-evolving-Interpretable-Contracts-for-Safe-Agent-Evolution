\section{Conclusion}
Agent evolution can expose consequences that an existing executable contract leaves unconstrained. We propose policy-guided contract co-evolution: use the supplied policy to identify relevant consequences, analyze effects and dependencies affected by an update, validate missing conditions, and incorporate justified ESL obligations while retaining prior protections.

This formulation gives a concrete procedure for narrowing contract-gap discovery. Whether it identifies enough useful obligations to prevent misbehavior is an empirical question. Our planned study reuses the existing Misevolution suite to measure gap discovery, prevention beyond fixed contracts with current instrumentation, and the utility and cost of the resulting interventions.
